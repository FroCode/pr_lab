### Slide 2: Project Vision & Goal
*   **Vision:** Democratizing preliminary healthcare diagnostics through generative AI.
*   **Core Goals:**
    *   Create a seamless bridge between patients and clinicians.
    *   Leverage real-time vitals tracking for informed AI analysis.
    *   Provide a premium, high-trust user experience.
*   **Speaker Notes:** Our goal was to create a platform that isn't just a symptom checker, but a holistic health management system that understands a patient's history and current vitals concurrently.

---

### Slide 3: Interdisciplinary Approach
*   **Medicine:** Clinical reasoning and symptom-to-condition mapping.
*   **Computer Science:** Generative AI (Gemini 2.5 Flash), state management, and async orchestration.
*   **Data Engineering:** Real-time visualization of multi-metric health data.
*   **UX Design:** Glassmorphic principles to reduce clinical anxiety and build trust.
*   **Speaker Notes:** This is an interdisciplinary project. We've combined clinical medical principles with cutting-edge AI and data engineering to ensure the results are accurate and the user experience is premium.

---

### Slide 4: Key Features - Patient Portal
*   **Real-time Vitals Monitoring:** Heart Rate, Blood Pressure, SpO2, and Blood Glucose.
*   **Interactive Analytics:** High-fidelity trend charts using Recharts.
*   **Appointment Management:** Integrated scheduling for specialist consultations.
*   **Consultation Archive:** Persistent history of all AI diagnostic results.
*   **Speaker Notes:** The Patient Portal is the heart of the app. It gives users a "Glassmorphic" view of their health, with real-time charts and a complete archive of their medical journey.

---

### Slide 5: Key Features - Symptom Intelligence
*   **Powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash:** Utilizing the latest in Large Language Models.
*   **Context-Aware Diagnostics:** The AI "reads" the patient's vitals (HR, BP, Trends) before making a judgment.
*   **Longitudinal Reasoning:** Correlates current symptoms with 10-hour health history.
*   **Safety First:** Automatic emergency flag detection for high-risk symptoms.
*   **Speaker Notes:** This is the "Brain" of MedCore. Unlike simple checkers, our AI is context-aware. It knows your heart rate and history before you even type a symptom.

---

### Slide 6: Technical Architecture
*   **Frontend:** React + Vite (High-performance state handling).
*   **UI/UX:** Vanilla CSS (Custom Glassmorphism Design System).
*   **AI Engine:** @google/generative-ai SDK.
*   **Data Layer:** Context Extractor for LLM prompt engineering.
*   **Speaker Notes:** Architecturally, we built a layered system. A React frontend feeds context into our "Context Extractor," which prepares a detailed medical snapshot for the Gemini API.
